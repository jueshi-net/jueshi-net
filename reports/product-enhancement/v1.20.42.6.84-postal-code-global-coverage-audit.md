## 5. Key Findings

### ✅ Strengths
- 43/51 supported countries have data (84% coverage)
- All data from single source (GeoNames) — consistent quality
- Most countries have 100% city/postal/coordinate coverage
- 71 additional countries have data but aren't exposed yet

### ⚠️ Issues
1. **8 supported countries have zero data** (VN, TW, SA, GR, IL, ZA, EG, NG)
2. **HK has only 1 record** — essentially unusable
3. **AE/SG/ID have no province data** — acceptable for SG (city-state), needs attention for AE/ID
4. **71 countries with data not exposed** — missed opportunity
5. **PH has 13% missing province** — minor issue

### 🔴 Critical Gaps
- **Vietnam (VN)**: 0 records — user-requested, major trade partner
- **Taiwan (TW)**: 0 records — major trade partner
- **Hong Kong (HK)**: 1 record — essentially broken
