# v1.20.42.18.4.1 Workspace Responsive Fix

**Created:** 2026-06-21  
**Status:** ✅ COMPLETED

## 问题描述

用户反馈工作台在窄宽下出现严重横向滚动：
- 顶部导航和右侧卡片在窄宽下撑爆页面
- 首页导航按钮被挤压成异常竖向/变形状态
- 出现横向滚动条

---

## 修复内容

### 1. Header 登录/注册按钮修复

**文件：** `src/components/layout/header.tsx`

**修复前：**
```tsx
<Link
  href="/login"
  className="flex items-center gap-1.5 px-4 py-1.5 bg-teal-600 text-white text-sm font-medium rounded-lg hover:bg-teal-700 transition-colors min-h-[36px]"
>
  <LogIn className="w-3.5 h-3.5" /> 登录 / 免费注册
</Link>
```

**修复后：**
```tsx
<Link
  href="/login"
  className="flex items-center gap-1.5 px-3 py-1.5 bg-teal-600 text-white text-xs sm:text-sm font-medium rounded-lg hover:bg-teal-700 transition-colors min-h-[36px] shrink-0 whitespace-nowrap"
>
  <LogIn className="w-3.5 h-3.5 shrink-0" /> 
  <span className="hidden sm:inline">登录 / 免费注册</span>
  <span className="sm:hidden">登录</span>
</Link>
```

**改进：**
- ✅ 添加 `shrink-0` 防止被压缩
- ✅ 添加 `whitespace-nowrap` 防止文字换行
- ✅ 响应式文字：桌面端 "登录 / 免费注册"，移动端 "登录"
- ✅ 调整 padding 和字体大小

### 2. Workbench 顶部区域修复

**文件：** `src/app/(workspace)/workbench/workbench-client.tsx`

**修复前：**
```tsx
<div className="flex items-center justify-between gap-3">
  <div className="flex items-center gap-3 flex-shrink-0">
    <div className="w-10 h-10 ...">
      {userInfo.image ? <img ... /> : initial}
    </div>
    <div>
      <p className="text-sm font-bold ...">{displayName}</p>
      <span ...>{role.icon}{role.label}</span>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div className="flex items-center gap-3 flex-shrink-0">
    <div className="min-w-[100px]">
      {/* 进度条 */}
    </div>
    <Link ...>升级</Link>
  </div>
</div>
```

**修复后：**
```tsx
<div className="flex flex-col sm:flex-row items-start sm:items-center justify-between gap-3">
  <div className="flex items-center gap-3 min-w-0">
    <div className="w-10 h-10 ... flex-shrink-0">
      {userInfo.image ? <img ... /> : initial}
    </div>
    <div className="min-w-0">
      <p className="text-sm font-bold ... truncate">{displayName}</p>
      <span ...>{role.icon}{role.label}</span>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div className="flex items-center gap-3 w-full sm:w-auto">
    <div className="min-w-[100px] flex-1 sm:flex-none">
      {/* 进度条 */}
    </div>
    <Link ... className="... flex-shrink-0">升级</Link>
  </div>
</div>
```

**改进：**
- ✅ 添加 `flex-col sm:flex-row` 移动端垂直排列，桌面端水平排列
- ✅ 添加 `min-w-0` 防止 flex 子元素撑爆
- ✅ 添加 `truncate` 让长文本截断
- ✅ 添加 `w-full sm:w-auto` 移动端占满宽度
- ✅ 添加 `flex-1 sm:flex-none` 进度条移动端自适应
- ✅ 添加 `flex-shrink-0` 防止按钮被压缩
